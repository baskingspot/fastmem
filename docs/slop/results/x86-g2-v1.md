## Findings

1. Above 1 KiB the kernels are at parity with glibc. Moves above 16 KiB on
   Intel are 3.3x faster than glibc (0.30): the forward-overlap cases in
   which glibc takes `rep movsb` (baseline finding 5).
2. memset is 8x to 30x faster than compiler-rt above 64 bytes.
3. The C-ABI kernel is slow at 0-16 bytes: 1.2x to 1.6x glibc, and 1.4x
   to 2.3x compiler-rt. The inline layer, which has the same size classes,
   is much faster than glibc on `dist/small` (0.38 to 0.78). The size
   classes are good. The cost is in the C-ABI entry: dispatch, prologue,
   and `vzeroupper` (unverified; the P3 follow-up measures it).
4. copy 17-256 bytes on c8i (1.31-1.39) and set 65-256 bytes on Intel and
   c7a (1.21-1.46) are the other gaps against glibc.
5. `dist/mixed` for set is slow on c7i (1.39) and c8a (1.77) against
   glibc, and copy `dist/mixed` is slow on the AMD targets (1.08-1.11).

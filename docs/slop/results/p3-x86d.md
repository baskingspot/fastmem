## Decision

- Take straight-1k on SPR and GNR: `-Dx86-variant=auto` now resolves to
  straight_1k there. It lowers 257-1K copy (c7i 1.15 to 1.09) and move
  (1.05 to 1.01), and c7i copy 0-16 B against compiler-rt (0.99 to 0.84).
- Rework medium-first: it fixes c8i 65-256 B (copy 1.28 to 0.99, set 1.32
  to 0.97) but costs 0-64 B (copy 0-16 0.85 to 1.09). The next candidate
  keeps the medium dispatch and leaves the small entry as it is.
- Drop ymm-medium.
- New after the layout fix: c7a copy 0-16 B is 1.35x compiler-rt on main.
  The earlier 0.86 (p3-x86c) came from a label-confounded run.
